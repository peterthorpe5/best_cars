# Electric, plug-in hybrid and hybrid evidence

The dedicated page uses a conservative reviewed catalogue in
`src/best_cars/data/propulsion.tsv`. Each entry has a manufacturer reference and
an attribution note. Unreviewed model identities remain unclassified, not
assumed petrol or electric. This initial catalogue is not exhaustive.

Dedicated series include reviewed Tesla models, Nissan LEAF, Volkswagen ID.3
and ID.4, Hyundai IONIQ 5, the explicit Prius Plug-in Hybrid identity and Prius+.
The generic Prius, Corolla and original IONIQ are mixed-family context entries.
Their pooled rates cannot identify a particular powertrain; mixed entries are
excluded by default. Prius+ is distinct from Prius and Prius Plug-in. Grouped
ID.4/ID.5 evidence remains excluded from model rankings.

Manufacturer descriptions establish eligibility, not reliability. Models may
still span generations, batteries and specifications. Generic IONIQ is placed
in the plug-in candidate view only as an explicitly mixed family; it is not
counted as a propulsion-specific plug-in estimate. Additional multi-powertrain
identities need a reviewed extension, not automatic substring matching.

The page retains sidebar evidence filters but has explicit page-specific source
and cohort-count thresholds, defaulting to one because coverage is sparse.
Original relative scores retain the full source peer population. Peer median
gaps also retain the complete currently filtered reference population. Scenario
ranks are restricted to common eligible competitors within the category and
cannot compare electric against petrol fleets or prove a propulsion advantage.

Inspection defects and roadside breakdowns do not measure traction-battery
state of health, capacity degradation, charging success, energy efficiency or
battery replacement costs. No such estimates are presented. A stronger future
comparison needs fuel/variant-resolved outcome counts, generation/registration
cohort identifiers, mileage and battery diagnostics. Current MOT aggregation
does not retain propulsion-specific outcome strata; no retrofit is claimed.

Review date: 12 October 2026. Manufacturer URLs are shipped in the TSV so the
eligibility decisions can be audited without silently changing saved outcomes.
